How to play

Move: arrow keys or W A S D on a keyboard, or swipe on a touch screen. All tiles slide as far as they can.

Merge: when two tiles with the same number touch, they combine into one tile with double the value, and you score that value.

Win: make a 2048 tile. You can keep playing afterwards. The game ends when the board is full and no tiles can merge.

Undo takes back your last move (one step). Your best score is saved in this browser.
